About the Festival

Raising a Bold Generation of Creative Christian Teens

Our Mission

To inspire and equip Christian teenagers to discover, develop, and deploy their God-given creative gifts as powerful tools to express their faith, communicate God's Word, and spread the Gospel.

"As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another." — 1 Peter 4:10

Our Vision

To raise a bold generation of creative Christian teenagers who know Jesus, discover their purpose, and use their gifts to reveal His beauty and impact their generation.

"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works." — Matthew 5:16

Our Simple Call

KNOW JESUS, DISCOVER YOU AND YOUR GIFT, CREATE BOLDLY AND IMPACT YOUR WORLD LARGELY.

"Let no one despise your youth, but set the believers an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity."

1 Timothy 4:12
